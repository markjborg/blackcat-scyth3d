# Quoting profiles

These JSON files are Ann's flattened OrcaSlicer 2.4.2 presets, copied from the
lab quoting toolchain. They are quoting defaults, not production-validated
print settings.

OrcaSlicer 2.4.2 has no official Bambu Lab H2C preset. The `h2c` pack is an
H2D 0.4 mm preset with the bed reshaped. The machine's own nozzle volumes are
left 325×320×320 mm and right 305×320×325 mm. Treat H2C slices as approximate.

P1S and X1 Carbon packs use a 250 mm slicer height even though the machines
are specified as 256 mm tall. The Giga pack is the Elegoo OrangeStorm Giga
0.6 mm preset. Exposure, flow, and support settings still need Mark's sign-off
before they are treated as shop defaults.
